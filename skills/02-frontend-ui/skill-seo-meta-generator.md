> **Standard Compliance:** AIMASTER Architectural OS Model

---

## 1. CORE PRINCIPLES & DOMAIN STANDARDS

### 1. OPENGRAPH & SOCIAL CARDS
- Provide full OpenGraph and Twitter card tags on every public route:
  - `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:url`, `og:image` (1200x630px recommended).
  - `twitter:card` (`summary_large_image`), `twitter:title`, `twitter:image`.

---

### 2. STRUCTURED DATA & CANONICAL HEADERS
- Embed schema-compliant JSON-LD structured data (`Organization`, `WebSite`, `Article`, `Product`).
- Ensure canonical URL tags (`<link rel="canonical" href="..." />`) are present on all routes to prevent duplicate content indexing.
